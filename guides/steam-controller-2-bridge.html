ACTIVE DEVELOPMENT · v0.8.0

Steam Controller 2
for Batocera

A userspace Bluetooth bridge that turns the Steam Controller 2's raw HID reports into standard Linux input devices on Batocera — without rebuilding or patching the kernel.

Where it stands

The project began as a way to make the Steam Controller 2 usable over Bluetooth on Batocera while the controller is handled by hid-generic rather than the current Linux hid-steam driver.

By v0.8.0 the bridge provides a normal virtual gamepad, expanded controller buttons, force feedback, automatic reconnect handling, and a separate virtual mouse for the trackpads.

GamepadStandard controls, sticks, triggers and D-pad.
Extra buttonsQAM, L4, L5, R4 and R5.
RumbleLinux FF_RUMBLE translated to SC2 Bluetooth haptics.
MouseRight trackpad pointer movement; left trackpad physical click supplies left/right mouse buttons.

v0.8.0 trackpad layout

Right trackpad movement -> mouse pointer Left trackpad physical click on left half -> left mouse button physical click on right half -> right mouse button Virtual devices Steam Controller 2 Bridge Steam Controller 2 Mouse

The earlier extra analog trackpad axes were removed from the virtual gamepad. The right pad's raw absolute coordinates are now converted into relative mouse movement with basic noise filtering and smoothing.

Force feedback

The virtual gamepad exposes Linux FF_RUMBLE. The bridge receives force-feedback effects from games or emulators and translates them into Steam Controller 2 Bluetooth HID output report 0x80.

Left and right haptic channels are independently addressable. Development testing found a practical gain range up to 128, and zero-magnitude requests send a true stop packet.

Bluetooth identity

Bus: 0005 Vendor: 28DE Product: 1303 HID_ID: 0005:000028DE:00001303 Driver: hid-generic

The bridge searches for that HID identity rather than relying on a fixed /dev/hidrawX number. The Bluetooth HID identity has been independently confirmed on two separate Steam Controller 2 units.

Service and reconnect behavior

The Batocera service keeps the bridge out of the way when the controller is powered off and recreates it after the controller reconnects. Recovery logic can also reset the Bluetooth adapter after repeated reconnect failures.

/userdata/system/sc2bridge.py /userdata/system/sc2watcher.sh /userdata/system/services/SteamController2 /userdata/system/logs/sc2bridge.log

Enable it from Main Menu → System Settings → Services → SteamController2, or start it over SSH with:

batocera-services start SteamController2

Testing status

Development testing has confirmed standard controller input, expanded buttons, Bluetooth reconnect, force-feedback upload/play/stop/erase, real in-game rumble, and the v0.8.0 mouse functions on the development Steam Deck/Batocera system.

A second Steam Controller 2 has independently passed normal controller-function testing. Final independent testing of the new v0.8.0 mouse behavior is still pending, so this remains a pre-1.0 development release.
